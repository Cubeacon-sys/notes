\section{Mathematical Notions and Terminology}
\subsection{Sets}
\begin{definition}[Sets]
  A \textit{set} is a group of objects represented as a unit. Sets may contain the type of the object, including numbers, symbols and even other sets. The objects in a set are called its \textit{elements} or \textit{members}. Sets may be described formally in several ways. Such as
  $$S = \{7, 21, 57\}$$
\end{definition}

The symbols $\in$ and $\notin$ denote set membership and non membership. We write $7 \in \{7, 21, 57\}$ and $8 \notin \{7, 21, 57\}$.
